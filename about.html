Our Story

Founded with a passion for creating memorable moments, Edilight Events and Bakes has been serving the community with exquisite baked goods and impeccable event planning services.

What started as a small home bakery in 2015 has grown into a full-service bakery and event planning company. Our journey began when our founder, inspired by family recipes passed down through generations, decided to share the joy of homemade baked goods with the community.

Today, our team of expert bakers and event planners work together to ensure that every celebration, from intimate gatherings to grand events, is nothing short of extraordinary. We believe that every milestone deserves to be celebrated with something special, and there's no better way to mark a moment than with beautifully crafted baked creations.
