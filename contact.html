Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How far in advance should I order?

For custom cakes, we recommend ordering at least 2-3 weeks in advance. For wedding cakes, please order 3-6 months ahead. Rush orders may be available - contact us for details.

Do you offer tastings?

Yes! We offer complimentary tastings for wedding cakes and large custom orders. Contact us to schedule your tasting appointment.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely! We can create gluten-free, dairy-free, egg-free, and vegan options. Please let us know about any allergies or dietary needs when placing your order.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we offer delivery within a 50-mile radius. Delivery fees vary based on distance and order size. Free delivery available for orders over $200.

What is your cancellation policy?

Deposits are non-refundable. Cancellations made more than 7 days before the event date may receive a partial refund. Please review our full terms when placing your order.

Do you offer baking classes?

Yes! We offer group baking classes for various skill levels. Perfect for team building, birthday parties, or just learning a new skill. Contact us for class schedules and pricing.
